1. Introduction

Welcome to Slashboard ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Sinhala Keyboard application (v4.2.1) across Android Phones, Tablets, and Android TV devices handles information. Slashboard is architected from the ground up as a privacy-first, local-execution keyboard.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

As an Input Method Editor (IME), Android displays a standard system warning when enabling any third-party keyboard. Despite this generic system prompt, Slashboard NEVER collects, transmits, or sells:

  • Any text, characters, Singlish transliterations, or messages you type in any application.
  • Passwords, PINs, credit card numbers, banking data, or One-Time Passwords (OTPs).
  • Clipboard history, pinned clips, or Sri Lankan NIC numbers analyzed by the built-in NIC utility.
  • Invoices, customer details, or product catalogs created inside the In-Keyboard Mini-POS & Invoice Studio.

3. How On-Device Features Operate in v4.2.1

  • Word Predictions & Personal Dictionary: Learned words and next-word predictions are stored strictly inside your device's private application sandbox (LocalLearningStore) and can be cleared anytime from Settings.
  • Mini-POS & Invoice Studio: All business profiles, items, and generated receipts are saved locally in an on-device Android Room (SQLite) database. Invoices are shared only when you explicitly tap Share or Save.
  • Low-RAM Memory Engine: Our 2GB/3GB RAM optimizer automatically recycles bitmaps and clears transient UI caches in RAM without writing personal data to external storage.

4. Transparent Permissions Explanation

Slashboard requests only the minimum permissions necessary for features you actively choose to use:

RECORD_AUDIO (Microphone)

Used exclusively when you tap the Microphone icon for Sinhala or English Voice Typing via Android's native SpeechRecognizer. Slashboard never records audio in the background.

INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used strictly for: (1) User-initiated In-Keyboard Translation queries when you explicitly use the Translate bar, (2) Downloading optional Emoji Style Packs (iOS/Apple, Facebook, Twitter/X, or Google Noto) from Maven Central when you click Download in Settings (WhatsApp Emoji Style is pre-installed offline), and (3) Checking for official app updates via Google Play Core.

VIBRATE

Provides tactile keypress haptic feedback according to your slider setting.

5. Data Retention & User Control

Because all preferences, learned words, downloaded emoji packs, and Mini-POS records reside locally on your device, you have 100% control. You can delete individual learned words, delete downloaded emoji packs to free storage, reset keyboard settings, or clear all app data via Android System Settings or by uninstalling the app.
